About Us

Norse Audio is a sub-brand of Bladelius. The system is designed in Sweden.

The Norse Audio system is a modular high-fidelity setup: the X1 power amplifier, the X2 pre-amplifier and the X5 power supply. Each part is built to work in the same ecosystem, so a system can start with a single stereo amplifier and later become a dual-mono setup, without changing direction.

Chassis are machined from non-magnetic materials, to reduce electromagnetic interference and the effect of vibration, and to keep the performance stable.
