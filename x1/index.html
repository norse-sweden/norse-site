X1 Power Amplifier

The heart of the Norse system. A fully balanced amplifier that can run as a stereo unit or, with a second X1, as a dual-mono pair.

A rear-panel toggle switch lets you move between stereo and mono modes, so a system can begin with a single stereo amplifier and later become a dual-mono setup.

The X1 also has an adjustable bias toggle with two modes:

  • Normal Bias — increases Class A operation for a warmer, richer sound with exceptional linearity.
  • Low Bias — prioritises efficiency while keeping high-quality audio, for extended use or less demanding listening.

Simpler is better

The X1 uses a single voltage amplifier stage for the shortest signal path possible. It is a fully balanced design without a ground reference, paired with a high-performance DC servo that operates outside the signal path.

The input stage uses hand-matched, current-loaded JFETs in a foldback cascade arrangement, for linear performance at any output level. Combined with current feedback, the X1 is fast, stable and dynamic.

This power output will drive a wide range of loudspeakers, from compact bookshelf models to demanding floorstanders, with precision and control.

Specifications

  • Power output, stereo: 50 W per channel at 8 ohms; 100 W per channel at 4 ohms
  • Power output, mono: 200 W at 8 ohms; 390 W at 4 ohms
  • Rated distortion: less than 0.01%
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: more than 125 dB
  • Frequency response: 1 Hz to 100 kHz, +0/−3 dB
  • Bias modes: Low or Normal, via toggle switch
  • Configuration: stereo or mono, via toggle switch
  • Dimensions: 240 mm (W) × 67 mm (H) × 248 mm (D)
  • Weight: 6 kg
