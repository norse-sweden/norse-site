X2 Pre-Amplifier

A high-end discrete MOSFET preamplifier, with a fully discrete, DC-coupled signal path and a high-precision relay attenuator.

The X2 uses a pure single-ended MOSFET gain stage, carefully set up for exceptional linearity, ultra-low noise, and a natural, effortless sound. The signal path is fully discrete and DC-coupled throughout, with a current-loaded output stage for maximum linearity, preserving signal integrity while keeping the warmth and musicality of a well-executed single-ended MOSFET design.

Volume control is a high-precision relay attenuator that uses premium 0.01% tolerance metal-film resistors in a ladder, or switched-resistor, topology. The result is outstanding accuracy, perfect channel matching and complete transparency, with no loss of quality even at the lowest listening levels.

Connectivity

  • 4 pairs of balanced XLR inputs
  • 3 pairs of balanced XLR outputs, for bi-amping or for driving several power amplifiers and subwoofers at once

Refinements

  • Ultra-low noise power supply with several stages of regulation and extensive decoupling
  • Microprocessor-controlled relay switching for silent input selection and muting
  • High-quality input and output relays with gold-plated contacts
  • A short, minimalist signal path and premium-grade components
  • Remote control

This architecture is built for a wide dynamic range, strong channel separation, effortless micro-dynamics and precise control. It brings together the musicality of single-ended MOSFET operation with the accuracy, speed and drive of a modern high-end system.

Specifications

  • Topology: discrete single-ended MOSFET, DC-coupled
  • Volume: precision relay attenuator, 0.01% metal-film resistors
  • Inputs: 4 × balanced XLR
  • Outputs: 3 × balanced XLR
  • Remote control
  • Powered by the X5 power supply
